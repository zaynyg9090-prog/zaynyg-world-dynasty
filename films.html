The Zaynyg World Film Slate 🎬

Original stories, built from real life — an adult animated comedy, a kids' animated feature, a faith documentary filmed in real time, and a disaster-comedy. Every title is 100% original IP, created by Jose Quinones.

North Park: The Last Scheme 18+ animated feature

The flagship. Jose and Antwan — animated versions of two real Albany friends — run one last scheme from a Maguire Ave porch while a silent war between a dog and a cat rages in the background of every scene. Real-deal city comedy: project-kid roots, roast battles, dark humor, and a whole lot of heart. Now in animation production.

Micro Metamorphosis Machines kids' animated feature

Tiny machines, giant heart. Kid heroes and their transforming machine partners face the reignition of the Kindle Star in an all-ages adventure about courage and friendship. Script in development.

Brick by Brick faith documentary

The real story, filmed as it happens: a father of five builds a creative empire from zero — honestly, on camera, pre-revenue — to fund a church that never closes and help the poor and homeless. Treatment complete; filming the journey now.

Tsunamicane PG-13 disaster-comedy feature

A continent-sized super water tornado. A retired dealer turned good. A woman, her kids, and one never-before-seen underwater creature. Funny, heartfelt, action-packed — with a funny ending. Script complete.
